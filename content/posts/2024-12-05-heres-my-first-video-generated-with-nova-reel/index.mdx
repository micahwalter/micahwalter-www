Prompt: "Generate a video of a dog eating a slice of pizza"

Not bad! [Nova Reel](https://aws.amazon.com/ai/generative-ai/nova/creative/) is pretty interesting. It took just a few minutes to generate this 6 second clip. The Amazon Bedrock Playground in the AWS Console will automatically create an S3 bucket and use the `start-async-invoke` API method to generate the video in the background. From there you can easily download the file.

He looks so happy!
