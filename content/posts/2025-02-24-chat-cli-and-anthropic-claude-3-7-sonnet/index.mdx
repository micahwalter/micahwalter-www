Anthropic [released a major upgrade](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-3-7-sonnet) to their Claude Sonnet LLM today with version 3.7. Shortly after it was announced, [Amazon announced](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/aws/anthropics-claude-3-7-sonnet-the-first-hybrid-reasoning-model-is-now-available-in-amazon-bedrock/) availability via [Amazon Bedrock](https://aws.amazon.com/bedrock), and shortly after that I went and made sure it works with [Chat-CLI](https://chat-cli.chat/).

It works!

I didn't have to update any code at all as I am using Bedrock's Converse API. It just works as if it was always there.
