> The variety and scope of work involved as an SA is also very unusual. One day could be an architecture review, the next a security incident review with a CSO, the next a customer enablement session, the next public speaking. This is why I personally enjoy the work as an SA so much. I enjoy the variety and freedom to work in a way that I think will have the most impact.
