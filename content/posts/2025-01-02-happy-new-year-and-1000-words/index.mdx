Welcome to 2025. I've been off, and it's been great. I'm easing back into action, and looking forward to this new year. Lot's to do, and lots to write about.

One thing I've decided to do, is to spend more time focusing on things that make me happy. It's a New Years Resolution I tend to make each year, and somehow, by December, I feel the need to start it all over again.

This time, I've decided to pay more attention to some of my creative outlets like photography and writing. I'll be posting "[daily photos](https://www.micahwalter.com/tag/daily-photo)" kind of daily here. I'm getting my old camera back in action, and stirring up my Lightroom catalog.

Also, I've decided (somewhat hastily) to try out [Jami Attenberg's Mini 1000 project](https://mini1000.substack.com/p/mini-1000-starts-tomorrow), which kicks off tomorrow and runs for five days. I'll be writing 1,000 words a day for a short story I've been dreaming up for far too long, and I'll publish some of it on this Substack that has been sitting dormant for the past six years. You can sign up to follow along, if you dare, [right here](https://micahwalter.substack.com/).

Meanwhile, I hope you all had a restful break. What kinds of things are you looking forward to for the new year?!
