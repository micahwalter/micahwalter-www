If you have been following, I [previously wrote about](https://www.micahwalter.com/2025/01/happy-new-year-and-1000-words/) a writing project I did at the start of the year. It wound up being a lot of fun (and hard work) and resulted in something I am somewhat proud of. You can read all about it over on my [Substack](https://micahwalter.substack.com/), which I created just for these kinds of projects. You can also subscribe if you are interested in the next project I'll be working on this summer.

For now, I thought it would be nice to put all the photos from the story here. Each one was assigned to a different chapter in the short story, but they were all taken during the same walk along the beach in Tel Aviv back in January of 2003.

In one of the photos, you can zoom in and actually see the real Mike's Place!
